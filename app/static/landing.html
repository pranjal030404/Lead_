Everything in one workspace

From raw search results to signed clients

One tool replaces the spreadsheet, the scraper tabs and the "did we already contact them?" group chat.

Searches that never repeat

Every search is logged by niche, city and area. Results are deduplicated automatically, so you never pay twice for the same businesses.

Scoring you can trust

Each lead is scored 0-10 on website quality, contactability and signals — then sorted HOT, WARM or COLD so you call the right people first.

Enriched automatically

Phones, emails via Hunter.io, website checks and opening-hours data are attached to every lead the moment it's found.

Outreach with a human gate

Messages are drafted from your templates, then queued for approval. Nothing is ever sent without you pressing the button.

Coverage at a glance

A live map shows every lead and every searched area, so you can see exactly which parts of a city are fresh and which are exhausted.

Built for a team

Multi-user accounts with roles, subscription plans that gate searches, and usage reports — your whole studio works from one pipeline.

How it works

Four steps, running while you sleep

Point it at a market

Pick a niche, a city and a max result count. Scheduled target runs fire on their own, too.

It finds & scores

Businesses are pulled from your data provider, enriched, deduplicated and scored automatically.

You approve outreach

Personalised messages sit in an approval queue with the lead's full profile one click away.

Pipeline does the rest

Track replies, meetings and deals through a visual pipeline with follow-up reminders built in.

Your server, your data

Self-hosted in minutes. Yours forever.
